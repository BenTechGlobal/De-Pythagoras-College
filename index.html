About Us

A Tradition of Excellence Since 1998

De-Pythagoras College is a co-educational secondary school dedicated to providing a balanced education that develops the mind, body, and character of every learner.

Our mission is to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to excel in higher education and contribute meaningfully to society.

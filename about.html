About De-Pythagoras College

Excellence • Integrity • Innovation since 2022

Our History

Founded in 2022, De-Pythagoras College began with a vision to provide quality secondary education that balances academic rigor with character development. Over more than two decades, we have grown into a respected co-educational institution serving students from diverse backgrounds.

Mission

To provide a holistic education that develops intellectually capable, morally upright, and socially responsible young people prepared for higher education and lifelong contribution to society.

Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized for academic excellence, innovative teaching, and the formation of principled leaders.

Core Values

  • Excellence — We pursue the highest standards in teaching and learning.
  • Integrity — We act with honesty, fairness, and accountability.
  • Innovation — We embrace creative approaches and technology.
  • Respect — We value every individual and foster inclusion.
  • Service — We encourage contribution to community and nation.

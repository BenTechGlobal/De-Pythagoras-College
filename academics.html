Academic Programmes

A rigorous, well-rounded curriculum for every stage of secondary education

Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3)

Students receive a broad foundation across core and elective subjects, building literacy, numeracy, scientific thinking, and social awareness. Assessment includes continuous assessment and end-of-term examinations.

Core subjects include: English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Social Studies, Civic Education, Computer Studies, Creative Arts, and more.

Senior Secondary (SS 1–3)

Students specialise in Science, Arts, or Commercial tracks in preparation for WAEC, NECO, and tertiary education. Subject combinations are guided by career aspirations and aptitude.

Sciences

Mathematics, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science

Arts

Literature, Government, History, CRS/IRS, Fine Arts, Languages

Commercial

Economics, Accounting, Commerce, Business Studies, Marketing

Enrichment & Extracurricular

Beyond the classroom, students participate in coding clubs, robotics, debate, drama, music, sports, and community service. These programmes develop leadership, teamwork, and practical skills.

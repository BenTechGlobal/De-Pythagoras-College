Admission Requirements

Applicants are expected to meet age and academic criteria appropriate to the intended class of entry. Typical requirements include:

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school reports / transfer certificate
  • Passport photographs
  • Entrance assessment (where applicable)
  • Interview with parent/guardian

Application Process

  1. Obtain and complete the application form (online or from the admissions office).
  2. Submit required documents and pay the application fee.
  3. Attend the entrance assessment on the scheduled date.
  4. Participate in a parent/student interview if shortlisted.
  5. Receive admission offer and complete enrolment formalities.
